Two kinds of account

Everyone shares the same five tabs along the bottom — Weather, Fields, Guide, Alerts, Settings. What changes is who can create alerts and manage the shared guide.

🧑‍🌾

Farmer

What you get by signing up
  • Check the weather and soil forecast for your location
  • Create your own farms and draw fields on the map
  • Browse the cultivation guide for your crops
  • Receive alerts and see them in the Alerts tab
  • Can't create or edit alerts
🛡️

Admin

Set up by another admin
  • Everything a farmer can do
  • Create and send alerts to farmers
  • Add incidents, hazards, risks and guidelines to the Guide
  • Edit or remove any alert
  • Can't be created from the sign-up screen
1 Get in

Create your account

The first screen you see is the login screen. New here? Switch it to sign-up mode and you're a farmer in two taps.

  1. Open the app. You'll see the Muhfarming logo and the heading Welcome back.
  2. Tap New farmer? Create an account at the bottom. The heading changes to Create your account.
  3. Type a Username and Password. Use the eye icon to check your password.
  4. Tap Sign Up. You're taken straight to the Weather screen — you're in.
Already have an account? Leave it on Welcome back, enter your details, and tap Log In. The app remembers you next time.
Admins: there's no role picker here. Signing up always makes a farmer — an existing admin creates admin accounts for you.
2 Set up your land

Add a farm

A farm is the home for your fields. You reach farms from the Fields map — they're not a bottom-bar tab.

  1. Open the Fields tab (the map icon in the bottom bar).
  2. Tap the farm icon in the top-right corner — Manage farms. This opens My Farms.
  3. Tap the ＋ button (Add farm). A sheet titled New farm slides up.
  4. Enter a Farm name and tap Save.
Your first time, the screen reads “No farms yet — Tap + to create your first farm.” Each farm you add gets a pencil to rename and a trash icon to delete.
3 Draw your land

Add a field on the map

Fields are drawn right on the map by tapping their corners. Any farmer can add one — no admin needed.

  1. On the Fields map, tap the Add Field button (bottom-right ＋).
  2. A banner reads Tap the map to select field corners. Tap at least 3 points to outline the field, then tap Continue.
  3. In the Add New Field sheet, pick a Region, type a Field Name, and choose the Farm it belongs to.
  4. Add any Notes if you like, then tap Add Field.
No farm to choose yet? Pick Add new farm at the top of the Farm dropdown to create one without leaving the form.
4 The main event

Send & receive alerts

An alert ties a hazard (an incident) to one of your fields. Admins raise them; every farmer sees them in the Alerts tab.

Admin sends it Admin

  1. Open the Alerts tab and tap the ＋ (Create alert).
  2. In Create alert, pick the affected Field.
  3. Pick an Incident — or tap Add incident to set its date, priority, and description.
  4. Tap Create. The alert goes out.
➜

Farmer receives it Farmer

  1. Open the Alerts tab.
  2. A new card appears with a priority pill, the date, and the hazard description.
  3. The bottom line shows Field: <name> • <region>.
  4. Filter by region using the chips up top. Pull down to refresh.
Good to know: only admins see the Create alert button and the edit/swipe-to-delete controls. Farmers just read — but they read every broadcast alert, so the whole community stays informed.
